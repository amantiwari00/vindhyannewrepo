Privacy Policy

How Vindhyan Geotechnical Services handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains what information this website receives when you contact us or apply for a position, and how that information is handled.

Information collected How we use it Email and service providers Storage and retention Cookies and technical data Your choices and contact

Information collected

If you use the contact form, the website receives your name, phone number, email address (if provided), selected service, and project message.

If you apply for a position, the website receives your name, phone number, email address, selected position, message, and the PDF resume you upload.

How we use your information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, understand service requests, review job applications, and contact you about your message or application.

Some submission details may also appear in the website application's operational logs while a request is being processed.

Email and service providers

Form submissions are sent to the VGS email address configured for this website using its Gmail email service. This means relevant information is processed by Google as part of email delivery and storage. The website does not sell submitted personal information.

The site requests font files from Google Fonts. When your browser makes that request, Google may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details.

Information may also be handled by the website hosting provider to operate and secure the site, or disclosed when required by law.

Storage and retention

The website does not have an automatic deletion schedule for form submissions. Messages and application materials may remain in the receiving email inbox, its backups, or service-provider logs until removed under the applicable retention practices.

Uploaded resumes are processed in memory and attached to the careers email; this website's application code does not save the uploaded file to its own disk.

Cookies and technical data

This website does not currently include an analytics or advertising-cookie script. Hosting and email providers may process basic technical information needed to deliver and secure their services. Your browser also controls any cookies set by other services you choose to use.

Your choices and contact

You can contact us to ask about information you submitted or request a correction or deletion. We may need to retain some information where required for business or legal reasons.

For privacy questions, contact Vindhyan Geotechnical Services at vindhyangeotech@gmail.com or +91 98341 18628.

We may update this policy when our website or information-handling practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
