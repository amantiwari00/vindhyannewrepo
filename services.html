Soil Mechanics Laboratory

We are associated with NABL-accredited laboratories and educational institutes to perform comprehensive soil testing. Every test follows IS standards ensuring accuracy and repeatability.

Our lab services include advanced testing for soil behavior under various conditions, including cyclic loading and environmental effects. We provide detailed analysis for foundation design, slope stability, and earthwork projects.
