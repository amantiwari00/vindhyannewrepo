About VGS

Founded in 2016, Vindhyan Geotechnical Services is India's trusted partner for Soil Investigation & Geotechnical Engineering.

Our Story

Founded by Mr. Sankatha Tiwari in 2016, Vindhyan Geotechnical Services has grown steadily to become one of India's recognized leaders in Geotechnical Engineering Services.

We excel in Soil Investigation and have executed projects pan-India across industrial, residential, commercial, and infrastructure sectors. Our in-house expertise and NABL-accredited lab associations ensure every solution is accurate and cost-effective.

We deliver on schedule and within budget — every time.
